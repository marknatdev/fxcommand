# The Pass Bar ignores win rate and judges each symbol separately

The owner once wanted a win rate of 70% or more. The Pass Bar deliberately leaves win rate out. A high win rate is easy to buy with a wide stop and a small target, and it says nothing about edge. The Pass Bar instead requires:
- walk-forward profit factor of at least 1.2 with at least 100 trades
- at least 5 of 8 profitable folds
- profit factor of at least 1.05 at twice the spread
- beating the 95th percentile of the Coin-Flip Baseline
- a Deflated Sharpe probability of at least 0.95
- a PBO of at most 0.3
- a clean Prosecutor

Win rate is still shown on the Verdict Card. A Hypothesis covers exactly one symbol on one timeframe. Pooling symbols would let a strong market hide a weak one.
