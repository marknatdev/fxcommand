Better Strategies for FXCommand: GOLD First

GOLD only. Two strategies (an H4 trend breakout and an intraday reopen drift), proven first on a notional Paper Account. A weekly Claude review hunts for upgrades, including multi-timeframe filters, behind a trial ledger and a sealed holdout. Supporting work: price-scaled costs, deferred entries around the daily break, and one Magic Number per Assignment.

How it works

Each strategy runs as its own Assignment with its own Magic Number. Signals that fall in the daily break become Pending Entries, sent at the first tradable tick within their fill window.

Two GOLD Assignments, two Magic Numbers, one shared entry path

GOLD and the index CFDs close for a daily break until 01:00 server time. A signal that arrives while the market or the Trading Window is shut is stored as a Pending Entry. It is re-checked by the Risk Gate against the first tradable tick and sent through the normal _enter path. Each strategy sets how long its Pending Entry may wait: 5 minutes for Reopen Drift, whose edge fades fast, and until the next bar close for the others.

What the GOLD research found

Intraday GOLD systems lose to spread except at the 01:00 reopen. H4 trend breakouts are positive overall but depend on the regime. Costs are scaled to GOLD's price each year.

Values are median mean R per trade in each period. About 400 GOLD variants were tested in total. Choosing the single most profitable backtest from that many mostly chooses luck, so these picks rest on consistency across variants and periods, not on peak profit.

  • Why intraday GOLD lost: XM's GOLD spread is a fixed ~$0.55. It was a large share of each small bar when GOLD traded at $1,200–1,800. The M1 bleed on your demo is this effect.
  • The reopen effect: GOLD's average rise per hour is largest in the 01:00 server-time bar, about +0.11 ATR in every period, and it is intraday, so it pays no swap. The spread at the reopen is ~$0.70, normal by 01:15, and by then the edge is gone.
  • Swap: XM charges ~$0.87 per ounce per night on GOLD longs, about 7%/yr at today's price, and pays ~$0.20 on shorts. Trend holds pay it; Reopen Drift never holds overnight.

Claude Strategy Review

Every Saturday Claude reviews the week's trades, tests fixes on history it is allowed to see, and scores finalists once on a sealed year. Only survivors become Challengers or PRs.

Every trial is counted, and the bar gets stricter as the count grows

  • Mistakes it looks for: stops hit right after entry, entries missed by the fill window, trades against the higher-timeframe context, costs above plan, and Paper drifting from the backtest.
  • Authority: Claude may submit Challengers, which go to Shadow and Paper under the existing Guardrails, and may open PRs for new code. It never merges, promotes, touches Sessions, risk or live trading, and never sends orders.
  • Objective: expected R after costs. Win rate is reported but never the target, because it can rise while profit falls.
  • Where results go: a Reviews page in the app plus a Telegram summary. PRs contain code only, because the repo is public.

Scalping (M1/M5/M15) is a research track on its own GOLD M5 Arena, separate from the H4/H1 strategies. Over 6.5 years of M1/M5 the M5 signals are about zero even before costs, and an M1 trigger makes every design worse. The one positive idea, a New York opening-range breakout, is a narrow peak: none of its 24 neighbours holds up in every period. Nothing trades there; the weekly review keeps watching it, and the 3-month scalping holdout stays sealed.

The dip filter is exactly the trap this loop guards against: it looked like a clear improvement on the data used to find it, and it failed on data it had never seen.

One Magic Number per Assignment

Two GOLD strategies can run at once. This supersedes ADR 0003, which already named this as its upgrade path.

  • Magic allocation: each Assignment has its own never-reused Magic Number. Ownership becomes (magic, symbol) per Assignment. The account is hedging, so opposite positions on GOLD can coexist.
  • Stable across edits: the magic is keyed by (Session, Symbol, Strategy, Timeframe), so editing a Session keeps it even though assignment ids are recreated.
  • Uniqueness: an Arena (Symbol, Timeframe) appears at most once among running Assignments, so Learning records keyed by Arena never collide.
  • Unchanged: the Kill Switch closes every position carrying any FXCommand magic. Orphan adoption, Pinned Login, Live Caps and the Paper routing table all move to per-Assignment magics.
  • Migration: an existing Session's current magic goes to its first Assignment; other Assignments get new ones.

Pending Entry lifecycle

Persisted and keyed by Arena and signal bar. It survives Interrupted, is cancelled by stop-type events, retries only transient rejects, and expires after its strategy's fill window.

Every final state is journaled once and written to the Learning Signal record

  • Key: (session_id, symbol, timeframe, signal_bar_ts, candidate_key). Never the assignment id.
  • Send path: only through SessionManager._enter, which keeps its guards: the one-call gate-and-send, _kill_seq, never opening while a close failed, one position per Assignment, and Order Outcomes. An uncertain entry is never retried.
  • Fill window: set per strategy. Reopen Drift uses 5 minutes, and the Journal records fill delay and spread. Other strategies wait until the next bar close. A paused Session never sends.
  • Deferred exits: exits that fall in the daily break are sent at the first tradable tick. Server-side SL/TP protect the position meanwhile.
  • Gold Reopen Risk Profile: a 100-point spread cap, because the reopen spread is ~70 points and the existing Gold profile caps at 60.

Paper Account

One notional balance shared by all Paper Sessions, with its own equity, day-start and limits. It is fully separate from the real account.

  • Equity: start balance + realized Paper P&L + open Paper P&L, including swap. The start balance is set on the Account page, e.g. $5,000.
  • Limits: sizing, risk %, daily-loss and global limits use the Paper Account. A Paper limit stops only Paper Sessions.
  • Isolation: the Paper AccountInfo is built outside gate_and_send and is never written into self._account. It keeps its own day-start key.
  • Reset: needs typed confirmation and is refused while Paper is active or open. Rows are archived with an epoch id. Paper tickets only go down, so they are never reused.
  • Swap and catch-up: swap on Paper positions comes from server-day rollovers, using the same function as the backtest. Catch-up pages through M1 bars, then falls back to H1/D1.
  • Statistics: Overview, History and Strategies split Paper Account from real account figures. ADR 0007 and CLAUDE.md are amended.

Costs, Cost Check and Evidence

Backtests charge spread and swap scaled to each bar's price. The Cost Check blocks strategies whose transaction costs eat the edge. Evidence shows whether the edge survives all costs.

  • Cost model: spread, slippage and swap per night are today's values scaled by close ÷ today's price. Triple-swap day comes from swap_rollover3days. One model version is used by Learning, Shadow Trades, the Evidence Run and Paper.
  • Cost Check: spread + slippage as R per trade, with the spread taken from ticks or the bar median. Start is blocked above 0.15R without a journaled per-Assignment override. It is enforced in _start and when a Promotion is applied. GOLD M1 costs ~0.3–0.5R and is blocked. Reopen Drift costs ~0.05R and passes.
  • Swap: shown next to the Cost Check but does not block. GOLD Trend pays ~0.1–0.2R per trade in swap and is still positive, which the Evidence reports.
  • Evidence Run: read-only, keyed by (symbol, tf, candidate_key, exit-rules hash, weekend_close, window hash, cost version). It reports mean R per period (2010–17, 2018–21, 2022–26), bars received and the date. Fetches are chunked and backtests run in the process pool. Results stay in the local DB only.
  • Scorecard: per Arena, the Paper record against the backtest's 90% band, plus real-account min-lot risk. Advisory only.

Learning

Learning uses the new costs, correct window timing, longer retention for slow Arenas, and family-scoped search inside narrow bounds.

  • Backtest timing: entries check the window at the next tradable time, using one helper shared with live Pending Entries.
  • Retention: keep at least 200 closed Shadow Trades per Candidate per Arena, whatever their age.
  • Search scope: the optimizer searches within the Champion's family, with separate search bounds. The noise and planted-edge tests are re-pinned. Guardrail constants are unchanged, and auto-promotion is never allowed on live.

Testing and verification

Everything order-related runs on SimBroker, which gains GOLD and index trading hours, swap and long history. The MT5 probe is read-only.

  • SimBroker: a daily break until 01:00, a wider reopen spread, swap fields and 600+ days of history. A new "Gold Reopen" Risk Profile.
  • pytest:
    • Magic per Assignment: ownership, migration, Kill Switch, Orphans, two GOLD positions at once.
    • Pending Entry states and the 5-minute fill window.
    • Paper Account limits and reset.
    • Price-scaled swap and spread, including the triple day.
    • Cost Check enforcement from the API, MCP, auto-resume and promotions.
    • Evidence keys, retention and family-scoped candidates.
    • Reviewer: the snapshot never contains holdout bars, every run is in the ledger, a holdout use can't repeat silently, and submit_challenger can't promote.
  • E2E and exploratory: two GOLD Assignments in one Session, the Evidence badges, the Cost Check block and override, the Paper Account, the stats split and the scorecard.
  • Read-only MT5 smoke: logs when the H1 and H4 bars appear after the reopen and GOLD's spread per minute from 01:00 to 01:15. Order methods stay tripwired.
